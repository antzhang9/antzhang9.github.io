🧋 The Boba Index

An unofficial cost-of-goods index, in the spirit of The Economist's Big Mac Index — but with bubble tea.

Snapshot for August 15, 2026 · prices and exchange rates as gathered that day

Methodology

The basket item is a regular/medium Pearl (Boba) Milk Tea from Gong Cha — a bubble tea chain operating in 20+ countries with largely standardized menus, making it a reasonable stand-in for the Big Mac's "identical product, everywhere" trick. Each local price was converted to USD at the day's market exchange rate, then compared against the U.S. price ($5.75) as the baseline — exactly how the raw (non-GDP-adjusted) Big Mac Index works. A negative number means the drink is cheaper in that country than in the U.S., after currency conversion; a positive number means it's pricier.
